# User Guide

Welcome to Bangla Call. Here's how to get your new `09649` number.

## Find a number

- Use the **Smart Number Finder** to generate options. Each result has:
  - **Copy** — copy the number,
  - **Use** — auto-fill it into the application form,
  - **Buy Now** — open a pre-filled WhatsApp message to book it.
- Or check a specific number's availability directly in the form.

## Apply

1. Fill in your **name** and **mobile number** using the on-screen keyboard
   (numeric for numbers, full keyboard for text). The focused field always stays
   above the keyboard.
2. Optionally enter a preferred `09649` number — availability and the required first
   recharge are shown automatically.
3. Choose **Personal** or **Corporate/PBX**.
4. (Optional but faster) Expand **Add live photo & NID** to capture a selfie and your
   NID with your camera, or upload images. These are stored securely and speed up
   activation.
5. Submit online, or tap **Register via WhatsApp** to send your details in chat.

## Good to know

- **Connection fee is free.** Your account activates on the first recharge (typically
  100 BDT for standard numbers, 500 BDT for premium patterns), and you get a 10% bonus.
- You can copy any number or booking details to share.
- Need help? Use the WhatsApp button or the hotline shown on the site.
